Lockout — Terms of Use

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Lockout blocks apps you choose, for a period you commit to, and deliberately gives you no way to unlock early. That is the product. Please read the two sections below before you use it, because they describe consequences you cannot undo once a lockout starts.

What you are agreeing to

By using Lockout you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, delete the app. Lockout is provided by AutomateEdge for personal use by adults managing their own device.

The lockout is intentionally irreversible

When you log a lapse, start a focus session, or trigger an automatic ban, the apps you selected are blocked until the period you chose has elapsed. There is no unlock button, no PIN, no override, and no customer-support escape. This is the entire purpose of the app, not a defect.

The only ways a lockout ends early are the ones built into it: reaching your step goal, or claiming a break with a qualifying workout.

Choose your blocked apps and lockout length carefully. Do not block anything you may urgently need. In particular, consider that you may need access during an emergency, at work, or while travelling. Lockout does not block the Phone app, Messages, or Emergency SOS, but you are responsible for what you choose to block.

It does not interpret them and draws no conclusions about your health. Do not use them to make medical decisions.
